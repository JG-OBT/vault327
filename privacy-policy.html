Our Story

Born inside a bank vault

The owners dreamed of opening a restaurant, but never imagined it would be inside an old bank. When they discovered this historic building on Crowthorne High Street — with its towering columns and vault door still standing strong — they knew it was fate.

Inside, the original bank vault now protects something far more valuable: a kitchen alive with creativity. The thick walls, once lined with safety deposit boxes, now resonate with laughter, clinking glasses, and the sizzle of Head Chef Mo's extraordinary dishes.
